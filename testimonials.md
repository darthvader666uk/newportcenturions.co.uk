          <p>I joined because of my friends (Duane and H) as they thought I would enjoy it. Not knowing much about the sport, I thought I would give it a go. Having been in sports teams previously, there was always a "macho" and "competitiveness" but with Korfball, and with the mixed sex element, it's totally different and welcoming. You can be as competitive as you want or, not at all and that's ok. Initially I was terrible BUT over time it was giving me what I was looking for; exercise with a purpose. Being a lot older (double the age of some of the youngsters) I wasn't sure if I really belonged here. I also had a game in my first season, pitted against an incredibly experienced player, that I was so poor, I wasn't sure I wanted to continue. The positive mentality of the club, brilliant help from my team mates taught me early on that not to fear and keep on going, believing in myself. Here I am, starting my 5th season, in my early 40's coming off the best season I have ever played, being the coaches male player of the year AND the people's champion. Roll on the next season and back to training :)</p>
